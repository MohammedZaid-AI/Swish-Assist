Customer sends a photoOn WhatsApp: "my fries are missing" plus a picture of what arrived.
Vision + fraud checksA Groq vision model compares it to the order. Duplicate photos, late claims and refund-heavy accounts are flagged.
Resolved or handed offSmall, confident cases are refunded instantly. The rest reach a human with a 10-second summary.
